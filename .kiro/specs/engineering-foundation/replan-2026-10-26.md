# Re-plan proposal, 2026-10-26

Status: **approved by the owner on 2026-10-26** (with option B for cryptography) and applied to
requirements.md, design.md, tasks.md and the steering files.

## Why

The owner's review on 2026-10-25 found that the foundation had grown out of proportion. There are about
1,000 lines of product code, none of it football yet, next to 2,500 lines of checks and hooks. Eight of the
remaining Phase 0 tasks are AWS and GitHub machinery that neither player needs before the advisor works.
The owner asked to reach a usable advisor sooner, to make the checks much faster (done: `make check` 135 s
to 66 s, the end-of-turn hook to about 15 s), and to leave small decisions to the agent.

## Principles from now on

1. **Smallest thing that meets the requirement.** No new tooling unless a feature needs it now.
2. **Decisions:** the agent makes small calls itself and logs them in the task report. A report asks the owner
   for at most three decisions, each with a recommendation.
3. **Speed is a requirement:** `make test-fast` under 20 s, `make check` around a minute. A slow check is a
   defect to fix, not a cost to accept.

## Then: one thin slice to "X or Y?"

Instead of writing Specs 1, 2 and 3 in full, one after another, the next spec (`mvp-x-or-y`) takes one
question end to end: **"X or Y?" for two forwards, answered in the owner's Claude Desktop.**

- **Data (Spec 1's first slice):** submit tools for probable lineups (two or three outlets), match odds
  (1X2 and over/under 2.5), and season stats and ratings for the players asked about, with the official
  player list for names. Bronze, silver and gold as in the skeleton.
- **Engine (Spec 2's first slice):** method §A3 and §A4 for forwards: team xG from the odds, goal and assist
  shares shrunk toward role priors, the flat cameo value, the ordering key. No simulation yet.
- **Tool (Spec 3's first slice):** `compare_players`, with a refresh plan when data is missing, and the agent
  prompt served by the MCP server; local, over stdio.

Later slices, in order: keepers and defenders, the full lineup with league rules and the bench, the
formation call, then Phase 3's online server (with the deferred AWS tasks) so the friend can connect.

acceptance tests pass. The slice keeps the order inside itself (data, then engine, then tool) but doesn't
